  \begin{itemize}

  \end{itemize}

    chains, code--test links) \textit{inside} its search loop with no new actions; built the \textbf{AST-based graph construction}.
    \item \textbf{+13.9 Acc@5} and \textbf{+12.2 Recall@5} over the strongest of \textbf{7 baselines} (incl.\ SWE-agent, OpenHands, Claude Code, Codex)
    across \textbf{4 benchmarks} spanning localization, test generation, and codebase QA.
  \end{itemize}
